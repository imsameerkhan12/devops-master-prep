# Day 15: Final Mock + Story Polish + Confidence

## 2-Minute Story Format

```
Hook (15 sec):     Set context fast — "We had 50+ services on Vault, license cost was growing..."
Action (75 sec):   What YOU did specifically — technical detail, decisions made
Impact (30 sec):   Numbers. Always numbers. "Zero downtime. $X/month saved. 50 services migrated."
```

---

## Tricky Questions — Pre-Loaded Answers

### "Biggest failure?"
- Use a REAL one (small scale is fine)
- Structure: what happened → what you did → what you learned → what changed
- NEVER: "I'm a perfectionist" or "I work too hard"

### "Why leaving current job?"
- Growth, learning, scale, new challenge
- NEVER: "manager is bad", "team politics", "salary low" (even if true)
- Template: "I've learned a lot at TokenTide — built infra from scratch, handled [X]. Now ready for [larger scale / more complex architecture / stronger engineering culture]."

### "Why should we hire you?"
1. Experience match: "I've done exactly this — EKS, Terraform, CI/CD at scale"
2. Hands-on: "I don't just design, I implement and operate"
3. Autonomy: "I can take ambiguous problem → solution without hand-holding"

### "5-year plan?"
- Be specific (not vague "grow")
- Option A: "Staff/Principal SRE — deepen platform expertise, influence architecture"
- Option B: "Platform Engineering Lead — own developer experience + infra platform"

### "Salary expectation?"
- Don't reveal first: "I'm looking for market-aligned compensation"
- Senior DevOps India 2025-26: 18-45 LPA (company-dependent)
- Negotiate ESOPs (most flexible part of offer)

## Questions to Ask Interviewer (Pick 3-5)

**Smart (shows depth):**
- "What's the team's biggest engineering challenge right now?"
- "How does the team handle tech debt vs new feature balance?"
- "What does the on-call rotation look like?"

**Strategic:**
- "What would success look like for this role in the first 6 months?"
- "What does the engineering culture look like day-to-day?"

**Casual:**
- "What do you personally enjoy most about working here?"

**NEVER ask in tech round:** hours, leave policy, salary (discuss in HR round only)

## Final 5 Things

1. **STAR + numbers** — always. Every story has impact numbers.
2. **Honest gaps** — "Haven't used it recently but concept is X, I'd pick it up fast." Don't bluff.
3. **Think loud** — for design/debug problems, narrate your thought process.
4. **Ask questions** — 3-5 good questions at end. Silence = disengaged.
5. **Pause is OK** — "Good question, let me think for a second" is professional, not weak.
