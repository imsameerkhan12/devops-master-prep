# Questions to Ask the Interviewer

> Ask 3-5 questions. These signal you're evaluating them, not just hoping they pick you.
> Pick questions relevant to what you learned during the interview — show you were listening.

---

## Engineering Culture

- "What does the on-call rotation look like? How many alerts per week on average?"
- "How are post-mortems run here? Blameless culture or still learning that?"
- "How often do you deploy to production? What does the deploy pipeline look like?"
- "When the last big outage happened — what changed as a result?"

---

## Team + Role

- "What does success look like for this role in the first 90 days?"
- "What's the biggest infrastructure challenge the team is working through right now?"
- "Is there a dedicated platform team, or is DevOps embedded in product squads?"
- "What would the person who was here before me say was the hardest part of this role?"

---

## Stack + Technical Direction

- "What's on the platform roadmap for the next 6 months?"
- "Are you on a monorepo or polyrepo setup? Happy with it?"
- "What's the current biggest pain point in your CI/CD pipeline?"
- "How much of your infrastructure is IaC today vs still manually managed?"

---

## Growth

- "How do engineers here typically grow — do people specialize deeper or move horizontally?"
- "What's the learning budget / conference allowance look like?"
- "Are there internal tech talks or architecture reviews I'd be expected to contribute to?"

---

## Never Ask in the Technical Interview

- Salary / comp (save for HR call)
- PTO days (save for offer stage)
- "Do you have any doubts about my candidacy?" (too needy, breaks the frame)
- "What does your company do?" (you should already know this — do your research)

---

## Quick Backup if Mind Goes Blank

> "What's the most interesting technical problem you've personally worked on here in the last year?"

This always works. Gets them talking. You listen and learn.
